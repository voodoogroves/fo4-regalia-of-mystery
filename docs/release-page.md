## Summary (short field)

Find a lost traveler's broken regalia in Boston, mend it, and wear the Order of Mysteries: blade, revolver, dress, veil and the Eye of Ra.

## Description

Someone from Appalachia didn't make it past Hubris Comics. Find what they carried, mend it, and wear it better.

Regalia of Mystery brings the Order of Mysteries gear, inspired by Fallout 76, to the Commonwealth. Each piece is found broken and repaired at a Chemistry Station. The notes you find teach the recipes; each one also needs a skill perk, shown at the station. There is one of each piece, so look after them.

**The pieces**

- **Blade of Bastet**: a golden sword that bleeds and cuts through part of a target's armor.
- **Voice of Set**: a silenced .44 that bursts with sound where it hits and may stagger.
- **Garb of Mysteries**: an armored dress for quiet work, worn under armor pieces.
- **Veil of Secrets**: helps you resist poison, and adds a persuade bonus (not yet confirmed in game).
- **Eye of Ra**: wear it and every other piece answers more strongly.

**Requirements**

Fallout 4 and the Nuka-World DLC. No script extender.

**When to install**

Can be added mid-game. In testing, adding it after a visit to the Hubris Comics area and Boston Common still spawned the items and containers.

**Install**

Use your mod manager, or copy `RegaliaOfMystery.esp` and the two `.ba2` files into the game's `Data` folder, then enable the plugin. It is a light (ESL-flagged) plugin.

**Compatibility**

No scripts. No vanilla item, perk or effect is changed. To place its objects the mod edits four cells and the Commonwealth worldspace record. Mods that edit the same places, including previs/precombine mods such as PRP, are untested and may conflict. Tested with Mod Organizer 2 on a Steam Deck, game version 1.11.240.

This is version 0.9: complete and playable, with polish still to come.
