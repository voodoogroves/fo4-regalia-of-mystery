Regalia of Mystery {version}
by voodoogroves

Someone from Appalachia didn't make it past Hubris Comics. Find what they carried, mend it, and wear it better.

Five pieces of the Order of Mysteries gear: the Blade of Bastet, the Voice of Set, the Garb of Mysteries, the Veil of Secrets and the
Eye of Ra. Each is found broken and repaired at a Chemistry Station.

REQUIREMENTS
Fallout 4 and the Nuka-World DLC. No script extender.

WHEN TO INSTALL
Can be added mid-game. In testing, adding it after a visit to the Hubris Comics area and Boston Common still spawned the items and containers.

INSTALL
Add this zip with your mod manager, or copy {name}.esp and the two .ba2 files into the game's Data folder.
Then enable {name}.esp. It is a light (ESL-flagged) plugin.

COMPATIBILITY
No scripts. No vanilla item, perk or effect is changed. To place its objects the mod edits four cells and the Commonwealth worldspace
record. Mods that edit the same places, including previs/precombine mods such as PRP, are untested and may conflict.
Tested with Mod Organizer 2 on a Steam Deck, game version 1.11.240.

PERMISSIONS
Free to use. Patches that require this mod are welcome. Please ask before re-uploading it or reusing its files, including for AI
training or forks. Never sell it or put it behind payment.

AI USE
Item names follow the lore. I wrote the diary, Cryptos and item descriptions from the lore, with AI help editing them and fitting
them to the page. Claude (Anthropic) wrote the scripts that generate
the plugin and textures. I directed the design and tested it in game. The scripts make the textures from Fallout 4 textures; no
image-generation model made anything in the mod. The Cryptos sketch is my own game screenshot run through ordinary image filters.
