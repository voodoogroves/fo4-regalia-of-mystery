Recovered notes from a collector of the Silver Shroud serials, on rebuilding the gear of the Mistress of Mystery. Find the broken pieces. Mend each at a Chemistry Station.

THE EYE OF RA. The gem is gone from its chain. Seat a new crystal in silver, and a rewired collar will hold it. It takes real science. Wear it, and every other piece answers more strongly.

THE BLADE OF BASTET. A blacksmith's work: silver and gold for the hilt, a crystal, an edge honed light. It finds the gaps in armor and bleeds what it cuts. Under the Eye, no armor stops it.

THE GARB OF MYSTERIES. An armorer can save the cloth. Wear it to move quickly and unseen, and to strike hardest from the shadows. Armor may go over it.

THE VEIL OF SECRETS. A chemist's mending. Behind it your words persuade, and poison loses its bite.

THE VOICE OF SET. A gunsmith can make it speak again. It makes no sound where you stand. It screams where it lands, and what it strikes may stagger.
