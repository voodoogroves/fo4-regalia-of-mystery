If you find this, I didn't make it. Please read it anyway.

I came up from Appalachia with what was left of her: a dress in tatters and an Eye with no light in it. We tried to make that wasteland better. Betrayal ended the first of us, and we've tried to pass the mantle on ever since. I meant for there to be more, but I'm the last.

I found most of what I came for. I couldn't keep hold of all of it.

Something big woke in the pond on Boston Common. I ran, hurt, and lost the Voice near the swan boats. I didn't go back.

I made it into Hubris and back out with the Cryptos notes, but I left the Eye and Veil behind. The ghouls inside were too tough and I had to escape to the roof. I stashed the Blade up there, across the jump. My leg won't make that twice.

I don't think I can do it alone. There's a building across the street and it sounds like there are people inside. I'll see if they can help me.

If I don't come back, take the pieces. Mend them. Wear them better than we did.

Someone should.
